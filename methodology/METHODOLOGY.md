# Methodology

## Research sequence

1. Define explicit business acceptance requirements before executing defect scenarios.
2. Separate business truth, destination representations, adapters/mappings, frozen rules and expected results.
3. Use deterministic outcome states: PASS / FAIL / UNKNOWN / APPROVED DIFFERENCE.
4. Preserve raw evidence pointers and avoid guessing missing identities or facts.
5. Freeze requirements/rule families before testing later representations.
6. Record coverage gaps rather than expanding the rules after reveal.
7. Separate technical-mechanism survival from commercial validation.
8. Run public-source incumbent/substitute research before authorizing an MVP.
9. Park the commercial hypothesis when buyer, budget and net-value evidence remain unresolved.

## Important methodological limitations

- synthetic environments rather than real customer systems;
- AI-assisted implementation/review;
- no true human manual baseline;
- no production Jira integration;
- no customer interviews or willingness-to-pay validation;
- Destination D author disclosed prior-context exposure, so strict clean-room independence is not claimed.
