THE BUSINESS PROBLEM

“The records moved” is not the same as “the business still works.”

Migration acceptance can depend on relationships, responsibility, historical authority, permissions, workflow actions and status meaning — not only counts or data presence.

01Identity

Did each business entity remain the same entity?

02Relationships

Did tasks, projects and clients retain the right links?

03Authority

Did permissions and approval authority remain correct?

04Behavior

Can required workflows still execute as intended?

THE MOST IMPORTANT RESULT

D-107 passed — and that was useful.

An incorrect project-accountability relationship was not caught because the frozen requirement set did not cover it. The rules were not expanded after reveal to make the result look better.

What the evidence supported

  • Recurring acceptance work exists
  • Expectations, exceptions, retest and sign-off recur
  • Reusable structures and evidence patterns exist
  • Paid migration/assurance services exist

What remained unproven

  • Material residual work after competent tooling
  • Separate software buyer and recurring budget
  • Net effort reduction after setup/maintenance
  • Standalone willingness to pay
