# Prototype 1 - Business-Function Migration Acceptance Research

**Status:** PARKED - SERVICES / INTERNAL-TOOL HYPOTHESIS PENDING FUTURE HUMAN VALIDATION

**Evidence scope:** Synthetic technical experiment plus public commercial research. No customer interviews, production migration deployment or validated standalone SaaS demand.

## Executive summary
Prototype 1 studied reusable acceptance evidence for repeated business-function migrations, ultimately bounded to specialist Jira Data Center to Jira Cloud migration delivery using JCMA. A synthetic mechanism survived bounded testing, but public evidence showed that migration tools, partners, testing practices and normal UAT already cover substantial portions of the workflow. The strongest commercial interpretation was an internal/partner delivery improvement, not a proven standalone product.

## Research question
Can reusable, explainable migration acceptance evidence reduce repeated validation work enough to support an independent product rather than an internal delivery method?

## Findings

### Technical finding
The synthetic technical mechanism survived bounded testing. This supports scoped technical feasibility only.

### Workflow finding
Recurring migration acceptance work exists: defining expectations, reviewing evidence/exceptions, retesting and sign-off.

### Commercial finding
Existing migration tools, integrators, partners and testing practices already cover substantial parts of the job.

### Best-supported interpretation
Potential internal or partner delivery improvement inside an established migration method; standalone software demand remains unproven.

## Decision
PARK. Do not build a standalone commercial MVP without future human evidence showing meaningful residual clerical work, repeatable buyer budget and net effort reduction beyond competent alternatives.

## Resume-safe framing
Independent technical-commercial research: designed and evaluated a bounded migration-acceptance evidence mechanism, then ran multi-phase public commercial falsification against native tools, integrators and testing workflows; parked the standalone SaaS thesis when residual demand and budget remained unproven.

## Evidence boundaries
- Do not claim universal migration correctness.
- Do not claim production Jira migrations were automatically validated.
- Do not claim customer demand, pricing or willingness to pay.
- Do not treat synthetic mechanism survival as proof of commercial value.
