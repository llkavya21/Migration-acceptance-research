# Claim boundaries

## Supported

- The project demonstrates a bounded synthetic mechanism for explicit migration-acceptance requirements.
- Phase 3 reports reuse of all 12 frozen business requirements and all 12 frozen rule families through Destination D with destination-specific adaptation.
- The synthetic work exposed both covered failures and an important uncovered defect (D-107).
- Public research supports that migration acceptance activity exists and that incumbent tools/partners cover substantial portions of it.

## Not supported

Do **not** claim:

- universal migration correctness;
- all defects were detected;
- production Jira migrations were automatically validated;
- clean-room or fully independent validation;
- an independent human review baseline;
- production readiness or enterprise deployment;
- statistical accuracy or industry benchmark performance;
- customer demand, revenue, pricing, ROI or willingness to pay;
- that a standalone services or software business was validated.

## Human baseline

The true human manual baseline remains **NOT RUN**. The C review was AI-assisted blinded review and must not be presented as evidence of human productivity savings or superiority to human acceptance testing.

## Commercial status

`PARKED — SERVICES / INTERNAL-TOOL HYPOTHESIS PENDING FUTURE HUMAN VALIDATION`

The absence of public proof of standalone demand is not proof that no opportunity exists. It is a reason not to build yet.
