# Prototype 1 — Final status

Closure date: 22 September 2026.

**PARKED — SERVICES / INTERNAL-TOOL HYPOTHESIS PENDING FUTURE HUMAN VALIDATION.**

Project: Business-function migration acceptance testing.

## Findings at closure

- **Technical finding:** the synthetic technical mechanism survived bounded testing. Frozen requirements and rule families operated through alternative synthetic representations using representation-specific adapters. This supports scoped technical feasibility only.
- **Public-evidence finding:** recurring migration acceptance work exists, including specifying expectations, reviewing evidence and exceptions, retesting and sign-off.
- **Incumbent finding:** existing tools and partners already cover substantial portions of this work. Business acceptance is not an entirely missing discipline or layer.
- **Unestablished commercial claim:** standalone-product demand is not established. Separate budget, willingness to pay and material net work reduction beyond competent alternatives remain unresolved.
- **Best-supported interpretation:** a possible internal or partner delivery improvement, within an established migration method. This remains a hypothesis, not a validated services business.
- **Development gate:** future human validation is required before commercial development. Access to evidence alone does not establish a reason to build.

## Scope retained

The commercial question is bounded to specialist teams repeatedly delivering native Jira Data Center → Jira Cloud migrations using JCMA. It concerns agreed acceptance requirements and evidence review, not universal migration correctness.

## Operational disposition

Active work is stopped. There is no Phase 4D, MVP, production integration or customer contact under this disposition.

Parking is an uncertainty-management decision. Lack of public evidence is **not proof that no opportunity exists**. Nor does uncertainty justify continued development.
