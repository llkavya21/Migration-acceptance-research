# Prototype 1 — Prohibited claims

Closure date: 22 September 2026.

These limits apply to interviews, presentations, portfolio material and any future commercial discussion.

| Do not claim | Accurate alternative |
|---|---|
| Universal migration correctness or complete business continuity | Verification was limited to explicit acceptance coverage in synthetic experiments |
| All migration defects were detected | In-scope effects were detected in the bounded D adjudication; D-107 exposed an uncovered accountability defect |
| Perfect independent or clean-room validation | D was separately authored with disclosed prior-context exposure |
| Independent human review proved superiority | C used AI-assisted blinded review; the human manual baseline remains NOT RUN |
| Statistical accuracy, generalized precision/recall or industry benchmarks | Results describe this small controlled experiment and its stated denominators |
| Production-ready, enterprise-ready or proven real-SaaS performance | No production integration or customer deployment was tested |
| Representation-independent means integration-free | Frozen logic was reused with destination-specific mappings, adapters and probes |
| Low measured human engineering effort | Human active time was not measured; recorded intervals were session wall time |
| PASS means every business requirement is satisfied | PASS concerns the declared rules and evidence only |
| UNKNOWN is effectively a pass | UNKNOWN remains unresolved |
| Approved differences are ordinary passes | Authorized differences remain explicitly distinct |
| Missing identity mapping proves deleted data | Unmappable evidence is distinct from proven absence in a complete fixture |
| Migration acceptance is an entirely missing market layer | Competent native tools, partners and UAT already cover substantial portions |
| Customers need or will pay for a standalone product | Standalone-product demand and separate payment are unestablished |
| A services business has been validated | Internal/partner delivery improvement is a commercial interpretation pending human validation |
| Time savings, ROI, customer results or environmental savings | These benefits were not measured or established |
| No opportunity exists because public proof is missing | Public evidence leaves decisive commercial questions unanswered |
| Building is the next approved step | Prototype 1 is parked pending qualifying evidence and human validation |

Do not attribute all implementation work to the user personally. Describe AI assistance and the user's documented experiment direction honestly.
