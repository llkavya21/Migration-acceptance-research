# Prototype 1 — Evidence boundary

Closure date: 22 September 2026.

## What the evidence supports

| Evidence | Supported conclusion | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Bounded synthetic experiments | Explicit business acceptance logic can detect covered consequential failures in controlled representations | No real-SaaS integration, scale, security or production reliability established |
| Destination D reuse | Phase 3 reports 12/12 requirements and 12/12 frozen rule families unchanged | Destination-specific mapping, adapter and probe work was still required |
| Phase 3 scoped results | No identified in-scope false PASS or false FAIL under the recorded adjudication | The answer key supplied business effects; requirement verdicts were adjudicated after reveal, not an exhaustive pre-authored verdict oracle |
| D-107 | Incorrect project accountability passed because it lay outside explicit frozen coverage | All-PASS cannot mean universal continuity or absence of business defects |
| Public commercial evidence | Acceptance activity and repeatable delivery structures exist | Material residual cost and unmet demand after competent tooling are unmeasured |
| Existing alternatives | Native reports, partner methods and testing tools overlap substantially | Neither universal incumbent adequacy nor universal inadequacy is established |
| Commercial interpretation | Internal/partner delivery improvement is the best-supported hypothesis | Separate buyer, budget, adoption and scalable revenue remain unvalidated |

## Methodological qualifications retained

1. Destination D was separately authored, but its author disclosed prior project-context exposure in that session. Strict clean-room independence cannot be claimed.
2. The C-A–C-D review was AI-assisted blinded review, not an independent human manual baseline. The true human manual baseline remains **NOT RUN**.
3. Recorded D work intervals were session wall time. Human active time and AI active time were not measured; line counts are not productivity measures.
4. Synthetic native observations are not independently attested production logs. Controlled clone-based action probes do not prove live concurrency, persisted end-to-end behavior or production safety.
5. UNKNOWN means unresolved evidence. APPROVED DIFFERENCE means an explicitly authorized, verified exception; neither silently becomes PASS.
6. Proven record absence in a declared-complete fixture differs from unidentifiable or unmappable evidence. Missing reconciliation alone does not prove deletion.
7. Phase 4 evidence is dominated by public documentation and supplier material. Templates establish structure, not adoption rates; advertised service scope is not awarded spend or willingness to purchase another tool.
8. No environmental/resource benefit has been measured.

## What remains unknown

Residual activity-separated labor; net savings after setup and maintenance; reuse across comparable real engagements; accessible and trustworthy platform evidence; responsibility for assurance; buyer authority; make/buy preference; and sufficient remaining migration work for payback.

The synthetic study and public research answer different questions. Technical survival is not commercial validation. Missing public proof of demand is not proof of zero opportunity.
